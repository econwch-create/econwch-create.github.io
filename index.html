Welcome!

Canhui WEI

I am a PhD student in Economics at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China (UNNC), where I was awarded the fully funded FHSS Faculty Scholarship. My research interests lie in health economics, household finance, and applied microeconometrics.

About

My research uses rigorous empirical analysis to evaluate health care policies, especially health insurance policies. I hold a M.Sc. in Finance and Investment (Economics, Money and Finance) from UNNC and a B.Econ. in Insurance from Shandong University of Finance and Economics.
